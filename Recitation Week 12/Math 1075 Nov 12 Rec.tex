\section{Something Really Important} 
Recall that we have 
\begin{enumerate}
\item $(a+b)^{2}=a^{2}+2ab+b^{2}$; 
\item $(a-b)^{2}=a^{2}-2ab+b^{2}$; 
\item $(a+b)(a-b)=a^{2}-b^{2}.$ 
\end{enumerate} Now solve the following: 

\vspace{1.5em} 

1. $(3+\sqrt{2})^{2}$ 

\vspace{4.5em} 

2. $(3-\sqrt{2})^{2}$ 

\vspace{4.5em} 

3. $(3-\sqrt{2})(3+\sqrt{2})$

\vspace{4.5em} Do you notice anything special about some of these problems?
